01 Workflow

From a sampling point to a signed report

The order specialists already work in. Each record carries its context forward instead of being retyped into the next tool.

  1. Plan sampling

    Programs with locations, frequency, methods and reviewers.

  2. Collect in the field

    Samples recorded with custody details, also without a connection.

  3. Receive results

    Lab files checked for units and duplicates. Source values kept.

  4. Analyse and review

    Compare locations, screen unusual values, open investigations.

  5. Report

    Findings, charts and references. A named specialist approves.

Laboratory results

Source values are never overwritten

A correction is a separate entry with a reason and a reviewer. Every record keeps its custody trail and audit history.

Analytics

Flags that show their reasoning

Unusual values are screened against a location’s own history. Each flag lists the numbers that triggered it.

Technical reports

Drafts that wait for a specialist

Tables, charts and counts fill from the data. Findings and recommendations are written by you. Limitations such as missing observations are listed automatically.

03 Scientific integrity

Built to support specialist judgment, not replace it

Water quality decisions affect public health. AquaTrace organizes the evidence and leaves conclusions with qualified people.

AquaTrace records

  • Location, date, laboratory, method and unit for every value
  • Source results, with corrections kept separate
  • The reason behind each flagged value, with its numbers
  • Measured results, estimates, missing data and suggestions, kept apart
  • A named reviewer before any report is approved

Reserved for specialists

  • Declaring regulatory compliance or legal conclusions
  • Setting risk thresholds or scoring weights
  • Prescribing disinfection or treatment changes
  • Diagnosing public health risk from a single result
  • Inventing citations or measurements
